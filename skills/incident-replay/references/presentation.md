# Produce and check the video

Use the user's chosen style and [HyperFrames](hyperframes.md) for the editable composition, preview, validation and final render, unless the user explicitly selects another framework. Animate meaningful interactions: characters act, objects or information move, and the scene shows the result. Use whole-scene reframing or transitions when helpful. Camera movement across static illustrations alone does not fulfill a request for character or object animation.

Generate or record actual narration within the task's authorization. Measure the audio and time the scenes from it. Align subtitles acoustically to the spoken words or phrases and check cue order, pauses and scene transitions. Translated captions should follow the corresponding spoken phrases. Changing speech language requires new narration and timing. State explicitly if listening or acoustic alignment was unavailable.

Verify the delivered artifact:

- Confirm the video exists, has the intended duration and dimensions, and decodes successfully.
- Inspect encoded sequences for meaningful motion, scene changes and readable captions.
- Report visual review, audio listening and subtitle checks separately; name checks not performed.
- If an interactive companion was requested, check its navigation, evidence links and narrow-screen layout.

Keep the notes, reconstruction, DAG and scenario accessible beside the film. Deliver partial work as partial. Publishing, paid services and external writes follow the authorization in the user's task.
